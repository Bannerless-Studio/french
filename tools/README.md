# tools/ — builder inputs (dev/agent notes)

Purpose: everything the French pack build reads or writes, other than the
shared builder itself (`engine/tools/packbuilder`, `langs/fr.py`). Read this
before touching any file here; it says what is hand-maintained vs generated.

## Files

- `build_pack.py` — shim. Runs `python3 -m packbuilder build --lang fr --repo .`
  against `engine/tools` (or `$PACKBUILDER_PATH`). Hand-maintained, rarely changes.
- `requirements.txt` — hand-maintained. Shared builder deps plus the French
  spaCy model (`fr_core_news_lg` — large, not small; see "Known deviations").
- `gloss_overrides.json` — hand-maintained. 293 hand gloss fixes from the
  round-4 full A1/A2 read-through, plus fixes found while writing passages.
- `forced_a1.txt` — hand-maintained. A1 core word list forced into A1 (closed
  sets and multiword expressions live in `langs/fr.py` instead).
- `h_aspire.txt` — hand-maintained. Words with an aspirated h (le héros, la
  honte), from Wiktionary's "French terms with aspirated h" category plus
  halte, hors.
- `generated_examples.tsv` — hand-reviewed written example sentences (`"src":
  "gen"` in sentences.json), exempt from the drop-everywhere content filter.
  Used to refill a word left without an example after a content-policy
  removal (e.g. le suicide).
- `passages_src.json` — hand-maintained. Source text for the 60 reading
  passages; `packbuilder passages` builds `pack/passages.json` from it.
- `id_map_v1.json` — generated, then frozen. Maps (lemma, POS) to a fixed
  word id across rebuilds. Never hand-edit; never renumber.
- `REPORT.md` — generated on every full build by `build_pack.py`. Everything
  outside the `manual` markers is regenerated; the manual section (QA
  verdicts, check output) is preserved across runs.
- `REPORT_passages.md` — generated by `packbuilder passages`. Per-passage
  coverage numbers and QA notes.

## Rebuild

See CLAUDE.md's "Commands" section for the pinned invocations. Sources
download once into `.cache/` (gitignored) and are reused; the build is
deterministic, so a cached re-run reproduces byte-identical `pack/*.json`.
Tagging the ~377k Tatoeba sentences with the large spaCy model takes 6-9
minutes the first time and is cached; later runs take about 8 minutes, most
of it the corpus passes. `build_pack.py --stage {corpus,tag,lex,freq,words,all}`
runs up to one stage from cached intermediate output.

## How words, senses and links are chosen

The shared pipeline (corpus-tagged POS/lemma, frequency split over
in-context readings, sense ranking by English-translation overlap, second
entries for a distinct-sense second POS at ≥20% of tokens) is in
`engine/tools/packbuilder/README.md`. The French module (`langs/fr.py`) adds:

1. **Tokenizer.** Every clitic after a hyphen is split off (peux-tu,
   lève-toi, dis-le-moi, allez-y), the euphonic -t- is skipped, and a few
   compounds stay whole (quelqu'un, week-end, là-bas).
2. **Elision.** Elided forms read as their full word: j'→je, l'→le/la
   pronoun, qu'→que, s'→se/si, c'→ce, n'→ne, d'→de, jusqu'→jusque; t'as/t'es
   read as tu. Frequency-list elision fragments fold onto those words.
3. **Contractions.** au, aux → à; du, des → de.
4. **Articles.** Nouns show le/la/l' (l' + gender note before vowel/mute h);
   aspirated h keeps le/la; common-gender nouns show le/la; pluralia tantum
   show les; days/months are bare. `alt[0]` is always the bare lemma.
5. **Reflexive verbs** show as "se lever" when ≥60% of linked sentences carry
   a reflexive clitic in the right person; otherwise the base verb with a
   combined gloss.
6. **Multiword expressions** (parce que, quelque chose, il y a, est-ce que,
   s'il vous plaît, petit déjeuner, and more — see `MWES` in `langs/fr.py`)
   are one word each; their parts link nothing else.
7. **Tagger repairs** by context: subject-pronoun+noun-reading → verb,
   être+adjective-reading → adjective, personne in negative clause →
   "nobody", possessive+verb-reading → noun, sentence-initial imperative.
8. **Passé simple** sentences kept to B1 (tagger Tense=Past on a finite
   indicative verb, or Wiktionary-only-passé-simple/imperfect-subjunctive
   readings).
9. **Feminine forms** fold into the masculine unless they carry their own
   sense (la droite, la nouvelle).
10. **Excluded:** proper nouns, fillers/names read as words, unassimilated
    English loans, profanity (sentences with profanity are skipped too).
11. **Paradigms.** Determiners have one entry per paradigm (ce/cet/cette/ces,
    mon/ma/mes...); always-pronominal verbs shown with se.
12. **Lemma artefacts:** a participle whose verb is barely used is the
    adjective (désolé, enceinte); bel/nouvel/vieil → beau/nouveau/vieux;
    voilà/voici are interjections.
13. **Hyphenated compounds** ranked by subtitle frequency; wordfreq phrase
    frequency used only up to the subtitle value.
14. **Gender homographs** show the corpus-majority gender, naming the other
    gender's sense in the gloss.
15. **Example sentences:** at least one shows the bare lemma when available
    (`example_shows_word`). Sensitive-topic sentences (sexual content,
    drugs, suicide, threats, violence, death) kept to B1; rape/sexual-abuse
    sentences dropped at every level.
16. **Glosses and sensitivity:** vulgar sense never leads a gloss
    (`sensitive_gloss_re`); a word whose A1/A2 gloss has no clean sense left
    moves to B1 (`lower_level_gloss_re`).
17. **Reflexive senses** appended to a plain verb whose corpus uses often
    carry a reflexive clitic ("appeler = to call; s'appeler: to be called").

## Reading passages

`packbuilder passages .` builds `pack/passages.json` from `passages_src.json`
and writes `REPORT_passages.md`. It links word ids the same way as the
Tatoeba sentences, and enforces in-pack coverage of at least 95% at A1/A2
and 93% at B1, plus a level budget (an A1 passage may use at most 3 A2 words
and no B1 words; an A2 passage at most 3 B1 words). French-specific passage
linker rules (declared names never recased/linked, noun readings of finite
verbs in predicate position, été after en/l' as the season, multiword-span
handling) live in `langs/fr.py`'s passage hooks, not in this repo — see
`../TODO.md` for the full list. Regenerate `pack/*.js` afterward
(jsonify_pack.py appends passages into sentences.js).

## Known deviations from the ideal spec

- The tagger is `fr_core_news_lg`, not `_sm`: on a 3,000-sentence sample the
  small model mistagged most subject pronouns and the verbs after them; the
  large model scored 99.7%/96.9%. Same licence (LGPL-LR) and build speed.
- au/aux/du/des link to the preposition only (à, de), not also to the article le.
- Days and months are shown without an article.
- Sentences are 4-14 tokens (B1 at least 5); an A1 word with fewer than two
  such sentences may take a 3-token one.
- Typing stays accent-lenient (`ou` = `où`) but a fold-only match is
  rejected when it spells another pack word: la/là, sur/sûr, où/ou,
  côté/côte, marché/marche, élève/élevé, âge/âgé, each direction.

See `../TODO.md` for the running residuals list and `REPORT.md`/`REPORT_passages.md`
for full counts.
