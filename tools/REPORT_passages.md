## Manual QA (2026-09-24, first build; polish round the same day)

- **Source and build.** The 60 passages are hand-written for this pack. The
  authoring sources assemble into `tools/passages_src.json` (`"src": "gen"`).
  The builder writes `pack/passages.json` and this report. Two runs give
  byte-identical output.
- **Builder.** vocab-engine packbuilder at 3fb879c plus passage-stage French
  hooks (`langs/fr.py` passage_* methods, `langs/base.py` defaults,
  `passages.py` wiring). None of them changes the word or sentence build.
  The hooks: declared names are never recased or linked; a capitalised word
  that is not a declared name is read as the common word (Madame, Monsieur,
  un Espagnol, les jeunes Français, « Les gens », « J'ai »); a noun reading of
  a finite verb in predicate position is the verb (Le train part, et lit un
  livre, » demande Léa); été after en / l' is the season; a token the tagger
  left as X gets its dictionary class (dansé); a multiword expression spans
  all its parts (parce qu', Est-ce qu', au lieu du, grâce au, il y avait); an
  unresolved form links its pack base (amie, allemande); a noun with no pack
  entry does not fall back to a same-spelling word of another POS when the
  English names the noun's own sense (la ferme "farm" is not ferme "firm").
- **Questions.** Every question's `words` are linked in its `sentence`
  (297/297). The first build claimed that no true/false item copies its
  sentence; 9 did share a run of 5+ words with the text. They are rewritten,
  and no true/false item now shares a 5-word run with any passage sentence.
  Correct mc options that repeat 4+ words of their sentence verbatim: A1 5/50,
  A2 1/58, B1 0/58. The correct option is the unique longest in 20/166 (12%).
  Wh-questions use est-ce que at A1-A2 and inversion at B1.
- **Balance.** Correct mc answers sit at positions 0/1/2/3 as 42/42/41/41.
  True/false answers are 63 true and 68 false.
- **Out-of-pack lemmas.** salade (x2) and cuillère (x1) in the fruit salad
  recipe (p0025), and la ferme "farm" in p0044 and p0054. The pack's ferme is
  the A2 adjective "firm", so those tokens are left unlinked and declared.
- **Unlinked tokens, by design.** Beyond the out-of-pack words above: the
  euphonic -t- of "explique-t-elle", and the parts of hyphenated numerals
  (vingt-quatre, trente-cinq). Numerals are not counted for coverage.
- **Language notes not applied.** Words the review suggested that are not in
  the pack were not used: mi-temps (p0031), poissonnier (p0016), rallumer
  (p0040), aimable (p0058), centre-ville (p0001), selle (p0057 uses "sur le
  cadre"), oral (p0020 uses "comprendre les gens").
- **Glosses.** The review's missing senses were added to
  `tools/gloss_overrides.json`: cher dear, ensemble together, gagner earn,
  arriver à manage to, s'occuper de look after, le public audience, feuille
  sheet, marcher work, compris included, tenir run / y tenir, direction
  management, tout droit straight on, maître owner, amie, de partitive. ami
  keeps its "(m)" tag, which the French word check requires on l' nouns.
- **Known pack issues seen here.** The shipped Tatoeba sentences share some
  of the tagger faults fixed on the passage side: "fatiguée" links parler
  (a bad Wiktionary form-of entry), "le plus" links plaire, and "amie" is
  unlinked. Passages avoid "fatiguée"; the other two are handled.
